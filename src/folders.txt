# Virtual folders, in reading order: path | description
# Functions say where they live with `;@ path: folder/sub`; data blocks without a
# path go to the folder of the functions that use them.
system | Power-on, interrupts and the hardware services used everywhere.
system/vectors | The fixed addresses the CPU jumps to: restarts, interrupt vectors and the LCD hooks next to them.
system/header | The cartridge header: entry point, Nintendo logo, title and checksums.
system/boot | Power-on: memory, the OAM DMA routine, the LCD and the interrupts.
system/interrupts | The interrupt handlers: VBlank, the HBlank hooks that touch VRAM a tile at a time, timer and serial.
system/vblank | What happens in every VBlank: the sprites, the sound requests, the hooks.
system/joypad | Reading the buttons, with a history of the presses for the secret codes.
system/lcd | Switching the LCD off and on, clearing the background.
system/memory | Copying memory.
system/delay | The busy-wait delays that pace the game.
title | The title screen, the credits, the attract mode and the menus.
title/credit | The developer credit at power-on.
title/intro | The logo coming in and the wait for START.
title/screen | Drawing the title screen.
title/demo | The PLAY SPACE INVADERS screen with the score table and the upside-down Y.
title/menu | One or two players, and the number of cannons in a link game.
game | The game loop, the rounds and what comes between them.
game/main | The main loop of a round.
game/start | Starting a new game.
game/round | Starting and clearing a round.
game/pause | The pause.
game/cheats | The secret button codes on the title screen and in the game.
game/coffeebreak | The coffee breaks after rounds 10 and 20.
game/ending | The ending after round 30.
game/over | Game over.
game/screens | Screens between rounds and the wait for START.
invaders | The formation of 40 invaders.
invaders/setup | Where the formation starts in each round.
invaders/formation | The layout of the formation.
invaders/draw | Drawing the invaders into the background.
invaders/march | The march: one invader moves per step, the formation turns and comes down at the edges.
invaders/kill-codes | Secret orders of shooting the invaders that rebuild the formation, restore the shields or stop the bombs.
player | The cannon.
player/move | Moving the cannon and firing.
player/hit | The cannon is hit.
shots | The cannon's shot and the invaders' bombs.
shots/move | Moving the shot and the bombs; the homing ones of the secret modes.
shots/bombs/drop | Which invader drops which bomb, and when.
shots/hits | What a shot or a bomb hits.
shots/hit-invader | The shot hits an invader.
shields | The four shields, kept as tiles in RAM and eaten away pixel by pixel.
shields/init | Setting up the shields for a round.
shields/data | The shields' tiles and the crater masks.
shields/gfx | Copying the shield tiles to VRAM.
shields/hits | Checking bombs and the shot against the shields.
shields/craters | Shifting a crater mask onto the point of the hit.
shields/craters/bomb | Cutting a bomb's crater into one of the 24 shield tiles.
shields/craters/shot | Cutting the shot's crater into one of the 24 shield tiles.
ufo | The mystery ship.
ufo/timer | When the UFO comes.
ufo/spawn | Starting the UFO on one side.
ufo/move | Flying across.
ufo/draw | Its sprites.
ufo/hit | The UFO is hit.
ufo/score | What the UFO is worth.
score | The score, the hi-score and the cannons left.
score/score | Adding to the score.
score/hiscore | The hi-score.
score/lives | The cannons left and the extra cannons.
score/digits | Writing digits.
link | Two players over the link cable.
link/menu | Choosing the link game and the number of cannons.
link/start | Starting a link game.
link/sync | Keeping both Game Boys in step.
link/serial | The serial transfers.
link/round | Rounds in a link game.
link/lives | The opponent's cannons.
link/result | Winning and losing.
link/end | The end of a link game.
link/screen | Link-game screens.
text | Texts and how they are written.
text/draw | Typing a text letter by letter.
text/demo | The texts of the attract mode.
text/sprites | Texts made of sprites.
gfx | Graphics.
gfx/tiles | Tile graphics.
gfx/tilemap | Screens as lists of rows of tiles.
gfx/oam | Sprite tables.
sound | The sound engine, run by the timer interrupt, and its 29 sounds.
sound/engine | Starting sounds and the tick that plays them.
sound/march | The march beat.
sound/data | The sound table, note frequencies and the wave patterns.
sound/headers | One header per sound: which channels it uses and their streams.
sound/streams | The note streams, one per channel of a sound.
unused | Code and data nothing uses.
unused/hooks | VBlank hooks that are never selected.
